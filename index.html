About us

Wenseslaous and Happiness Royale is Dar es salaam most extraordinary fine dining destination, a modern space where timeless royal elegance meets bold, innovative cuisine.

Born from passion to create unforgettable memories of joy and luxury, we reimagine classic European grandeur with contemporary flair and East African hospitality. Every detail from our golden crest and sparkling chandeliers to our theatrical tableside presentations is designed to make every guest feel truly royal.

We source the finest ingredients from Tanzania and beyond, transforming them into signature dishes such as our renowned Golden Crown Scallops.

Paired with an exceptional wine selection, Wenseslaous and Happiness offers more than just a meal—it delivers a complete sensory experience.

Whether you are celebrating love, success or simply life’s beautiful moments we invite you to dine like royalty in the heart of Dar es Salaam.
